Calculator recommendation 
A graphing calculator is required for all mathematics courses. The TI-84 Plus CE graphing calculator is strongly recommended as the  
preferred calculator for all Stevenson High School mathematics courses. In addition, students will use a Stevenson-issued iPad to access  
notes, view feedback, engage in learning activities and collaborate.
